Method 2 of 3

Reselling on eBay and Vinted

Cost us £135.70
ResultDown £135.70, and 41 hours gone.

This worked brilliantly in 2014. It doesn't any more.

We spent £640 on stock from car boots and clearance aisles, listed 54 items, and sold every one of them for £812.50. Then eBay took its fees, Royal Mail took the postage, and the packaging took the rest. We finished £135.70 down.

The bigger problem was the time. 41 hours in a month, and none of it optional. Every sale is a photo, a description, three messages from someone offering half price, and a queue at the Post Office.

Verdict

It isn't a business. It's a shift you do at home, and you're bidding against people who do it full time.
